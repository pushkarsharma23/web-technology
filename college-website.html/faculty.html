Government College of Engineering and Technology (GCET), Jammu was established by the Government of Jammu and Kashmir in 1993 with the approval of the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and is affiliated with the University of Jammu. It was created with the vision of providing valuable resources for industry and society through excellence in technical education and research. The campus is spread over 869 kanals of land at Jammu, approximately 22 km from Jammu city. GCET Jammu has become the first institution in the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir to have all five of its undergraduate programs accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). The Institute has also been conferred Autonomous Status vide UGC Communication No. F.2-10/2023(AC-Policy) dated 03.10.2024, effective from the Academic Session 2024-25.
